Last Updated: October 2026

1. Campus-Only Eligibility

CampusSwap is exclusively for registered students and staff within participating college campuses. You agree to provide accurate information regarding your college affiliation.

2. Peer-to-Peer Marketplace

CampusSwap provides a peer-to-peer discovery platform connecting students within the same campus. We are not a broker, payment processor, or delivery service.

3. No Payment Processing or Delivery Guarantees

All transactions, price negotiations, inspections, and handovers take place directly between buyer and seller in person on campus. CampusSwap does not offer escrow services, payment protection, warranties, or delivery guarantees.

4. Permitted and Prohibited Items

Students may buy and sell student essentials (books, calculators, electronics, stationery, hostel goods, bicycles). The sale of prohibited items, hazardous goods, academic dishonesty materials, or illegal items is strictly prohibited and results in immediate account termination.

5. Campus Safety and Handover

We advise all students to meet in public, well-lit campus areas (such as the campus library, canteen, or department lobby) for physical handovers.
