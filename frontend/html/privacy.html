Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

CampusSwap is a campus-only peer-to-peer student marketplace. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how your privacy is protected.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, email address, phone number, college name, department, and academic year.
  • Campus Verification: College ID card image uploaded to verify student affiliation with your campus. Verification images are stored securely and reviewed by administrators for campus integrity.
  • Listing Information: Titles, descriptions, prices, handover locations, and photos of items you post for sale.

3. How We Use Information

  • To restrict marketplace access so that students only see listings from their own college campus.
  • To enable buyers and sellers within the same college to contact each other directly (e.g., via WhatsApp) for physical item handover.
  • To moderate content and maintain campus safety.

4. No Commercial Third-Party Sharing

CampusSwap does not sell, rent, or share student personal information with advertisers or third-party marketing companies.

5. Handover & Safety Disclaimer

CampusSwap does not process in-app payments or handle item delivery. All physical handovers and transactions occur directly between students in campus public spaces.
